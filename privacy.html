Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. This notice is written for UK GDPR.

Holistique UK is a trading name of WellGlow Ltd. Registered in England and Wales, company number 16173232. Registered office: 3rd Floor, 86-90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE. Email: hello@holistiqueuk.com. ICO registration reference: ZC012926.

WellGlow Ltd, trading as Holistique UK, is the data controller. We do not have a separate data protection officer. Please use the email above for any privacy question.

What this notice covers

This website, newsletter sign-ups, bookings, the WhatsApp community, and enquiries you send us.

What we collect, and why

Newsletter. The sign-up form asks for your email address and any name you choose to give. The form is provided by Brevo. We use those details to send the newsletter. The lawful basis is consent. You can unsubscribe in any email, or by writing to us. We then delete the address, or keep it only on a suppression list so that we do not email you again.

Bookings and enquiries. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp about an event, an appointment or the course, we keep your name, contact details and the message, so that we can reply and provide what you asked for. The lawful basis is taking steps towards a contract, or performing a contract.

Health information. If you choose to tell us about your health so we can decide whether a session is suitable, that is special category data. We use it only for that purpose, not for marketing. The lawful basis is your explicit consent. Please do not send medical records. You can withdraw that consent at any time, and we will not be able to use the information for future sessions.

Event tickets. If you buy a ticket on Dandelion, Eventbrite or a similar platform, that platform is its own controller for the purchase. They may pass us your name and email address so we can run the event. We use those details to manage your place and to contact you about that event.

WhatsApp community. If you join, we can see your phone number and the messages you send. Other members can see your messages and, depending on your WhatsApp settings, your number. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a community you asked to join. You can leave at any time. WhatsApp is provided by Meta, which processes the messages under its own privacy notice. That can include transfers outside the UK.

This website. There is no account, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The home page also loads animation code from cdnjs and jsDelivr. Those services receive your IP address when your browser requests the file. The newsletter form is hosted by Brevo in an iframe. If you use the form, Brevo’s own notice applies, and the form may set cookies.

Records. We keep a basic record of payments and refunds because we have to for tax, and so that we can deal with a complaint. The lawful basis is a legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in handling disputes.

How long we keep it

  • Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe.
  • Enquiries that do not become a booking: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Booking and event records, including any health notes you sent us: 6 years from the booking, then deleted.
  • WhatsApp: for as long as you stay in the chat. Messages already sent can remain on members’ phones after you leave.

Who else sees it

We use Brevo to send the newsletter. Brevo is based in the EU. Dandelion and Eventbrite handle ticket sales as separate controllers. WhatsApp handles messages you send through its app. Google, cdnjs and jsDelivr only see the technical request your browser makes for fonts or scripts. We may give a venue a guest’s first name for a door list. We do not give a venue your health information. We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, erase it, or restrict how it is used. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it. Where the law gives you the right, you can ask for information you provided to be sent to you in a portable form.

Email hello@holistiqueuk.com. We aim to reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would like the chance to put things right first.

Children

This site and these services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it.
