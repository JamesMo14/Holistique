Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

This policy covers event tickets sold through Dandelion, Eventbrite and similar platforms, acupuncture and cupping appointments, and the self-acupuncture course. If a ticket platform asks you to request a name change or a cancellation on their site, do that as well as emailing us. Your statutory rights are not affected.

If we cancel or change an event

If we cancel an event, you can choose a full refund of the ticket price, or credit for that ticket price towards a future Holistique event.

If we change the date, the start time or the venue, you can choose a full refund of the ticket price, credit for that ticket price towards a future Holistique event, or a place on the new date.

Credit is valid for 12 months from the date we confirm it by email. A booking fee the ticket platform keeps, and does not return, is not refunded by us and is not included in the credit.

If you cancel an event ticket

We do not give a cash refund when you cancel an event ticket.

  • 7 days or more before the event starts: credit for the ticket price towards a future Holistique event, valid for 12 months from the date we confirm it by email. A booking fee the ticket platform keeps, and does not return, is not refunded by us and is not included in the credit.
  • Fewer than 7 days before the start: no credit and no refund. You may transfer the ticket to another person if you email us at least 24 hours before the start and give us their name.
  • If you do not attend: no credit and no refund.

These tickets are for a specific date, so the usual 14-day cooling-off period for online purchases does not apply to them. If you are unwell, the same notice rules apply.

Acupuncture and cupping appointments

Appointments are arranged with Yvonne by email or WhatsApp.

  • At least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move the appointment: no charge. If you have already paid, the session fee is refunded in full.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice, or a missed appointment: the full session fee is payable, or kept if you have already paid.
  • If we cancel: you can choose a full refund or another time.

If you booked and paid online, and the appointment is more than 14 days away, you may cancel within 14 days of booking for a full refund. If you ask us to go ahead sooner than that, this cooling-off right ends once the session has taken place.

Self-acupuncture course

  • 14 days or more before the first session: a full refund.
  • Less than 14 days before the first session, if it has not started: a 50% refund.
  • Once the first session has started: no refund.
  • If we cancel the course: a full refund.

If you booked the course online, you can also cancel for a full refund within 14 days of booking, unless the course has already started because you asked us to begin inside that period. If it has started at your request, we keep a fair share for the sessions already given and refund the rest.

How to ask

Email hello@holistiqueuk.com. Tell us the event or appointment, the name on the booking, and whether you want a refund, event credit, or a transfer.

We aim to reply within 5 working days. An agreed cash refund goes back to the original payment method within 14 days. Event credit is confirmed by email.

See also our terms and conditions and privacy policy.
