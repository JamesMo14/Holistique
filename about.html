About Holistique

Meet Yvonne

Yvonne is a trained Gong Practitioner, offering deeply immersive gong baths that invite profound rest, energetic alignment, and inner stillness.

Her sessions guide participants back to the body's natural rhythm through the transformative power of sound.

Gong Practitioner  ·  Acupuncturist
Kensington, London

A practice rooted in sound

A grounded presence.
A space to rest.

She has studied with esteemed teachers, including the Cummings Twins. She has worked alongside Seth Newman (URUB Aerial Sound Healing), as well as taken part in overnight gong pujas with RogerGongs, Joanna GongFree, and Wiola Diamond heart. Yvonne brings a grounded presence, intuitive sensitivity, and a strong healing foundation to every sound journey she holds.

Beyond sound

A holistic approach to wellbeing

In addition to her gong work, Yvonne is also a qualified Acupuncturist, trained in both Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Five Element Acupuncture. A graduate of the Asante Academy of Chinese Medicine and a registered member of the Acupuncture Society UK, she integrates acupuncture to support a wide range of physical and emotional conditions, including stress, anxiety, sleep issues, hormonal balance, digestive health, and pain management.
