Overnight Sound Healing in London

Why overnight changes the experience

By Yvonne · Oct 1, 2026 · 5 Min Read

A Deep Rest Journey at Colet House

Some evenings end at 9pm with tea and a Tube ride home. Others ask you to stay

Holistique UK’s Overnight Sound Healing Journey on Saturday 17 October 2026 at 9:00pm is the second kind — a longer container for relaxation and healing through sound at Colet House, the beautiful venue near Barons Court that hosts several Holistique gatherings.

If a standard sound bath is a deep breath, an overnight journey is permission to stop holding your breath until morning.

Why overnight changes the experience

In a short session, part of you still watches the clock. Bags, trains, tomorrow’s emails — they hover at the edge of the mat.

An overnight format softens that edge. You settle knowing you don’t have to “come back” quite so soon. The nervous system often follows: first the fidgets, then the heavier limbs, then the kind of rest that doesn’t feel available at home between notifications.

You’re not booking entertainment. You’re booking uninterrupted recovery time in a held room.

Deep rest is a skill. Long containers help you practise it.

Colet House — a note on place

Colet House (151 Talgarth Road, London W14, near Baron’s Court) has become a familiar Holistique landmark for sound and retreat days. It’s a proper London wellness address: reachable, atmospheric, and suited to lying down with intention rather than rushing between Zoom calls.

Venue specifics and arrival notes will sit on your ticket — always check the latest on https://holistiqueuk.com/events.

What the night is for

The public framing is simple and true: a magical journey of relaxation and healing through the power of sound.

In practice, that usually means:A guided arrival into the space

  • Extended soundscapes — gongs and companion instruments weaving tone through the room
  • Long stretches where you can drift, dream, or simply notice breath
  • A soft morning-adjacent landing before you re-enter the city

Exact instruments and pacing belong to the facilitators on the night. Your job is to receive.

Sound through the long night

In shorter baths, the arc is compact: settle, receive, return. Overnight, facilitators can afford patience. Tones can rise and fall like weather. Silence can be left alone long enough to become its own instrument.

You might notice waves of alertness and drowsiness. You might dream with your eyes closed. You might remember a worry, feel it move through, and discover it has less grip at 2am than it did at 2pm. None of that needs fixing in the moment. The room is designed to hold it.

If you’ve only ever experienced sound healing as a polite 75 minutes on a weeknight, this is the format that shows you what “journey” was always hinting at.

Practical preparation

Before you book / before you go: Confirm the 9:00pm start on 17 Oct 2026 and any stated end guidance on the ticket. Ask yourself honestly if overnight group rest feels nourishing right now. Plan travel that doesn’t leave you panicked at 8:40pm.

What to pack: Comfortable layers and socks; an eye mask; water; any requested mat or bedding; minimal valuables and maximum softness. Eat enough beforehand that hunger won’t narrate the night, but not so heavy you feel stuck.

Who thrives at an overnight journey

  • People craving more than a teaser sound bath
  • Light sleepers who still want structured rest away from home habits
  • Anyone who’s tried a shorter Holistique sound evening and thought, I could have stayed

If the idea of overnight group settings brings up real anxiety, a daytime retreat on Sunday 25 October or a standard evening may be a kinder first step — and that’s wisdom, not avoidance.

Boundaries and comfort overnight

You’re allowed to leave if you need to. You’re allowed to sit up. You’re allowed to place yourself nearer a wall or an exit. Good rooms make that explicit so you don’t suffer politely.

If you live with trauma sensitivity around darkness, groups, or unpredictable sound, read the event page carefully and contact the team with questions before you pay. Holistique events are held with care; your pacing still matters.

Earplugs are rarely needed for gong work and can change the experience — ask the facilitator if you’re unsure. Eye masks are usually welcome. Phones on genuine silent, not vibrate.

Think of overnight sound less like a concert and more like being lent a safer harbour for a few hours. You bring courtesy; they bring the container; the instruments do the long conversation.

How this sits in the autumn arc

October at Holistique UK moves from playful (Paws & Sound, 10 Oct) into deeper rest (this overnight, 17 Oct) and then into a full Day Retreat on 25 October. November brings Christian Dimarco’s London cluster and the Sacred Sounds overnight.

You don’t need to attend everything. You might only need one night that reminds your body what unforced quiet feels like.
