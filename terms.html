We may ask you and your dog to leave if that is the safer or kinder choice for the room. If we do that because of the dog’s behaviour, the ticket is not refunded and no credit is given. If we cancel the event, or we change the date, the start time or the venue, you can choose a full refund or credit, as set out above.

Acupuncture, cupping and sound sessions

Acupuncture, fire cupping and sound sessions are traditional wellbeing practices offered for relaxation. They do not diagnose, treat, cure or relieve any medical condition. They are not a substitute for advice from your GP or another qualified healthcare professional.

Please tell us before a hands-on session if you are pregnant, have a bleeding disorder or a pacemaker, or have an infection or broken skin where a needle or cup would go. We may decline or change a session. That conversation is not a medical assessment.

Appointments are for adults aged 18 and over, unless we agree otherwise in writing before you book. Sessions are given by Yvonne Liang, a qualified acupuncturist and a registered member of the Acupuncture Society UK.

The course is education for your own personal use. It is not a professional qualification.
