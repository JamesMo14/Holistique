Who Is Christian Dimarco? The Melbourne Sound Artist Bringing Didgeridoo Journeys to London

A plain guide to his music, what his sound journeys feel like, and his four London dates from 19 to 22 November.

By Yvonne · Nov 9, 2026 · 4 Min Read

A plain guide to his music, what his sound journeys feel like, and his four London dates from 19 to 22 November.

Christian Dimarco, on the listing for The Sound Within. Photo: Holistique UK

If you’ve never been to a sound journey, here’s the simplest way I can put it: you lie or sit somewhere comfortable, close your eyes, and someone plays live music that’s built for listening, not dancing. You don’t have to do anything. That’s the whole point.

This month we’re hosting Christian Dimarco in London, and a few people have asked me who he is. So here’s what I know, without the hype.

Who he is

Christian is a sound facilitator, music producer and multi-instrumentalist from Melbourne. He’s been guiding sound sessions, meditations and workshops for more than ten years. He writes and produces his own music, and this summer he played at Medicine Festival UK.

His sessions mix didgeridoo, world flutes, drums, singing bowls and his own voice, with a little guided breathing woven in. A lot of his music grows out of meditation and the rhythms of nature.

In his words, he wants to “create a space where you can slow down, breathe, listen and reconnect with yourself, allowing the sound to become a journey rather than simply something you hear.”

What a sound journey can feel like

Everyone’s different. Some people drift off. Some feel emotional. Some just enjoy the music and go home feeling a bit lighter. There’s no right way to do it, and you don’t need any experience.

I’ll be honest about my own first one: at my first gong bath I saw dinosaurs, as if I’d time travelled. I’m not promising you dinosaurs. But I do think it’s worth giving your mind an hour with nothing to do.

Four ways to hear him in London

Christian is only here for four dates, and each one has a different feel.

  • The Sound Within, Thu 19 Nov, 7pm to 9pm, St Botolph-without-Bishopsgate (near Liverpool Street). A City church, mostly seated in the pews, with a few lying-down spaces. A good one straight after work. Tickets
  • Into Stillness, Fri 20 Nov, 7pm to 9pm, The Amadeus, Little Venice. You lie back in a chapel and let the week go. Your ticket also gets you into our holistic market downstairs from 6pm. Tickets
  • Sacred Sounds, Sat 21 Nov, 9pm until morning, Colet House, Barons Court. An overnight sound bath with Christian and other musicians: gongs, didgeridoo, harp, piano and singing bowls. You bring your bedding and sleep inside the sound. Tickets
  • Medicine Music, Sun 22 Nov, 4pm to 6pm, AndSoul Sanctuary, Cheshire Street (walkable from Shoreditch High Street or Bethnal Green). A lying-down Sunday reset before the week starts. Tickets

Booking soon? Use the code FRIEND at checkout. All four dates are on Christian’s London page.

I’ll write a separate piece on how to choose between them later this week.

Dogs are welcome, but you don’t need one, and children are welcome too. The next ones are Mon 9 Nov and Wed 11 Nov, with the sound part from 11am to 12pm. Book here

A few practical tips

  • Eat something light beforehand and drink some water.
  • Wear comfy clothes. Layers are good.
  • Arrive early. For the evening concerts the doors open 15 minutes before, and they close once the music starts.
  • Leave a little quiet time afterwards. A slow walk home beats checking your emails on the Tube.

Sound journeys aren’t a medical treatment, and they don’t replace your GP. They’re simply a lovely way to rest.
